Every demo follows the same 7-stage structure. Trades demos are 15-20 minutes. Medical and aesthetic demos can run 30-45 minutes (more polish required).

0-2

Rapport + Recap

Before diving in, do a quick recap of what they're dealing with. Use their exact pain points from the discovery call. Get them nodding before you go further.

2-5

The Cost Of Missed Calls

Walk through THEIR specific math. Use the 76% missed call stat applied to their volume. Translate it into dollars per month they're losing. Make it real.

5-10

Live AI Demo

Call your demo number. Let the prospect hear the AI handle a realistic call for THEIR business type. This is the wow moment. Stay quiet during the call. Silence sells here.

10-13

Show The Dashboard

Screen-share the dashboard. Show them what they'd see Monday morning — every call, every booking, every transcript, every dollar captured.

13-16

Pricing Reveal — Anchor With Elite

Three tiers. Elite at $697 for high-volume operators. Growth at $497, the most popular pick. Starter at $397 for solo operators. Setup is $997 to $1,497. Watch their reaction carefully.

16-18

Match Them To A Tier

Based on volume and needs, recommend ONE tier with a clear reason. "For your size, Growth makes the most sense because [specific reason from their situation]."

18-20

Close

"If we set this up this week, when do you want it live — Monday or Wednesday?" Assume the close. Hand them a Calendly link or take payment over the phone.

The Five Rules Of The PureCode Pitch

These are the rules our top closers follow on every call. Break any one of them and the close rate drops. Memorize these. Live by these.

01

Lead with the outcome, not the feature.

Wrong: "We have automated review requests."
Right: "Most service businesses get 1-2 reviews per month. Our clients average 8-15. That's how you outrank competitors on Google."

02

Always anchor with Elite.

Present all three tiers. Mention Elite first. Growth becomes the obvious value pick when Elite is the anchor. Without the Elite anchor, Growth feels expensive. With it, Growth feels like a deal.

03

Show their math, not generic stats.

"76% of trades calls go unanswered" is forgettable. "You're losing $76,000 per month at your call volume" is unforgettable. The dollar figure must have their name on it.

04

Don't ask: "Do you want to sign up?"

05

Dig one level on hesitation.

"I need to think about it" almost always equals an unaddressed objection. Always respond: "Of course — what specifically would help you decide?" Then handle whatever comes back.

Internal FAQ

The questions our team asks most often. Know the answers cold so you never have to put a prospect on hold to check.

What's actually in the $997 setup fee?

Custom AI voice training on the client's business, knowledge base build (services, pricing, hours, FAQ), CRM integration, calendar integration, phone number provisioning, testing, and the onboarding call. Roughly 8-12 hours of work on our side.

Why is Elite's setup higher ($1,497)?

Elite gets the dedicated success manager kickoff call (45-60 min), AI review response training on their existing reviews, voicemail drop scripts custom-recorded, and priority queue placement during build.

Can clients downgrade or cancel mid-month?

Yes — month-to-month after setup. They keep the current tier through the end of the billing cycle, then switch. No early termination fees.

Can clients upgrade mid-month?

Yes — upgrades take effect immediately. Prorated for the remaining days. New features activate within 24-48 hours.

What does "1 line" actually mean?

One dedicated business phone number that the AI answers. If they want a 2nd line for a 2nd location, they need the Multi-Location add-on.

What about texting from the AI?

Outbound text-back (missed-call) is included on all tiers. Full SMS conversations from the AI are included from Growth tier upward (under Saved Client Info).

Can the AI make outbound cold calls?

No — that's a separate product. This is inbound AI receptionist only. Don't confuse these in pitches.

What's the refund policy?

30-day money-back guarantee on the setup fee. Monthly fees are non-refundable but cancel-anytime.

Can the AI book to multiple calendars?

Yes — Growth and Elite tiers support multiple calendar integrations (e.g., one per technician).

Does it integrate with [common CRM]?

Native integrations: GHL (default), HubSpot, Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, AccuLynx (roofers), JobNimbus, Acuity, Calendly. Other systems via Zapier.

Can I offer a discount to close a deal?

Setup fee discount up to $200 — OK without approval. Monthly fee discount or a free month — requires approval. Default position: don't discount, add value instead (e.g., free bilingual for 90 days).

What languages besides English/Spanish?

Spanish is the only built-in second language. Other languages are custom builds with additional setup fees. Refer these up before quoting.
